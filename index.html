Yes, absolutely. Cancel anytime from your account page with no questions asked. You keep Pro access until the end of your billing period.

One run is one use of a tool — one resume scored, one cover letter generated, one email polished. Free users get 5 runs per tool per day. Pro users get unlimited.

Yes. Your resume and job description data is sent to generate your result and is never stored, sold, or used to train AI models. Your information stays yours.

ChatGPT is a blank canvas — you have to know the right prompts. Utility Forge AI is a purpose-built system with each tool optimised for a specific job seeker outcome. Less guessing, better results.
